  1. Soldering first (a mobile-repair shop, ~10 min): pins on the mic and amp boards, speaker wires to the amp's + −.
  2. Board unplugged. Connect the mic: 6 wires as in the picture. L/R goes to GND so the mic sends on the left channel.
  3. Connect the amp: 5 wires. VIN goes to 5V, not 3V3, for full volume.
  4. Check every wire against the table once more. A wrong 5V wire can damage the mic: the mic must be on 3V3.
  5. Plug the board's UART port into this Mac with a USB data cable. "My devices" above shows board found.
  6. Step 1 · Install MicroPython, then 2 · Set up Dikkey (your wifi name and password).
  7. 3 · Test: Speaker beep, Mic test, LED colours. Then Restart toy.
  8. Hold BOOT and talk. When it works, unplug from the Mac and plug the board into the power bank: it talks to Dikkey's brain over wifi. Keep the Mac on.
  9. On/off: use the power bank's button: press once to turn Dikkey on. To turn off, try a double press or a 3-second press; if the board's light stays on, unplug the cable instead. Only if that's a nuisance, add a USB cable with a switch (optional).

Toy board (ESP32-S3) looking…

Plug the board into this Mac with a USB data cable. This page sets it up for you; it works only on this Mac.

Your board: buttons, USB and pins

Hold BOOT to talk. Plug the Mac into the USB port (right). Mic power comes from 3V3, the amp from 5V.

Speaker + header pins

Your small speaker can't plug into the board directly. It goes to the MAX98357A amp, and the amp goes to the board.

  1. Header pins: break the strip to fit the amp (7 holes) and mic (6 holes), short side through the board, long side up. Solder each pin and check no two touch.
  2. Speaker: cut off the damaged white plug, strip 3 mm, solder or screw red to + (SPK+) and black to − (SPK−) on the amp.
  3. Never connect the speaker straight to a board pin.
  4. Amp to board: VIN→5V, GND→GND, BCLK→GPIO 15, LRC→GPIO 16, DIN→GPIO 17. Then use Speaker beep in step 3 below.

SD card (offline stories) optional · wiring only

For a toy that plays stories, songs and the anthem with no Wi-Fi. Use a micro SD card module (SPI, 4 signal pins) and a FAT32 card of 32 GB or less. A USB stick will not work on this board.

Mic wiring (INMP441)

Solder these six wires before plugging in. VDD must go to 3V3, never 5V.

After wiring, use 🎤 Mic test in step 3 below to confirm it: it tells you straight out if a wire is wrong.

1 · Install MicroPython (only once per new board)

Erases the board and installs MicroPython . Takes about a minute. If it can't connect, hold the board's BOOT button while pressing RST, then try again.

2 · Set up Dikkey

The toy joins your home wifi (2.4 GHz) and talks to Dikkey's brain on this Mac. The password goes only onto the board.

3 · Test

After setup: hold BOOT on the board, talk, let go. The LED shows green listening, orange thinking, purple talking, red a problem.

Tests stop the toy; press Restart toy afterwards so it listens again.
